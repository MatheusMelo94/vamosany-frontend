The menu on your phone

Today's phone menu is the desktop mega-menu poured into one column. Below are four working phones: today's menu and three ways to reorganise it. Tap the menu button, open groups, and switch the page you're "on". Desktop stays exactly as it is.

What's wrong today

Measured at 360 × 780 from the code, not guessed.

32links with no page yet
3rdscreen before Services
22pxrow height (44 needed)
  1. Everything is expanded

    5 groups, 14 small headings and 40 links, all visible at once. There is no first level to scan.

  2. What sells is buried

    Services, the only priced items (from €25, €149, €290), starts on the third screen. Descriptions and the "Most popular" tag are dropped on phones.

  3. Most links go nowhere real

    32 of 40 items (all Itineraries, Guides and Blog) fall back to the Services section of the home page. Layout alone can't fix that.

  4. Language and account are last

    After 40 links. The chips are 33px tall and ordered EN · ES · PT with EN selected, while the site is Spanish-first. Sign up and Log in lead nowhere yet.

  5. Too small to tap

    Rows are 22px tall; the invisible tap area only reaches 30px, not 44.

  6. Hard to close, hard to use without sight

    Close is top-right only, Esc doesn't close it, focus isn't trapped, and group titles aren't headings, so a screen reader hears 40 links in a row.

Recommendation: A · Índice

B is the natural next step once Itineraries, Guides and Blog have real pages.

  1. Fixes the disorganisation with the smallest change to the frozen design: same panel, position, radius, veil, type and yellow dot.
  2. A short, calm first level: five closed groups instead of 40 open links, so the menu reads as an index, not a wall.
  3. Removes the scroll wall: the first level fits on one screen; language and account are pinned at the bottom.
  4. Makes 44px real on the visible row, closing QA-0004 and RV-0006 properly.
  5. Lowest risk to build: one open/close pattern the FAQ already uses, and it works without JavaScript.

Decisions for you

A works whichever way these go.

  1. The 32 placeholder links

    Keep them (recommended for now), hide them on phones until their pages exist, or make each group header point to its one real page. This is the real root cause and deserves its own decision.

  2. Language order

    Show ES · PT · EN (Spanish first, as the constitution says) now, or wait for the translation work? The mockups show ES · PT · EN.

  3. Log in / Sign up

    Keep both, keep one, or hide them until the traveler dashboard exists? Today they lead nowhere.

  4. Tablet (768)

    Same full-width panel, or cap it at about 420px under the menu button? Recommended: cap it.

  5. Price style

    Small Montserrat (today) or Courier caps like the brand's labels? The mockups use Courier.

  6. A small motion

    Let the yellow dot "arrive" when the group you're in opens, using the landing's subway ease? Optional.
