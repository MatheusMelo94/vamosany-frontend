## Accessibility Results
- **axe violations (serious/critical) in the feature's pages and states:** 0.
  - Covered: 5 pages × 3 viewports idle; both forms × Idle, Field error, Not configured, No-JS.
  - The only contrast hits are the **known, allowlisted footer captions** (4.48:1, `docs/qa/known-a11y-2026-09-23.md`, ADR-0006). Lighthouse flags the same nodes. Not new.
- **Manual keyboard pass: DONE** (Playwright-driven, 1280 and 360):
  - **Navbar:** Tab to "Sobre mí" → Enter opens → Tab walks `/sobre-mi`, `/colaboraciones`, `/trabaja-conmigo`, `/contacto`, `/preguntas-frecuentes` in order. Each item shows a 2px solid focus ring, the marker follows focus, and `aria-current="page"` is on the current route. Esc closes and returns focus to the trigger. The panel now sits right after its trigger in DOM/Tab order, where on `main` Tab went to EN/Log in/Sign up first, so this is an improvement.
    - Observation, not a bug (pre-existing): Tab past the last item leaves the panel open while focus moves on into `main`. On all 5 pages no focused element was covered by the panel, so there is no 2.4.11 failure.
  - **FAQ:** Enter/Space toggle and focus stays on the summary (`faq.spec`). Deep links open the question below the sticky navbar.
  - **Forms:** Tab order is md order on both pages. No focused field is obscured by the sticky navbar at 360/768/1280. On error, focus goes to the first invalid field. On a valid submit, focus goes to the banner (`role="status"`), and the **next Tab lands on its `mailto:` link** (spec § Accessibility keyboard path).
    - Whitespace-only Nombre/Mensaje is rejected. An email with surrounding spaces is accepted. `maxlength="100"` on Nombre truncates silently, which is acceptable.
    - Every form control is 16px, so there is no iOS focus zoom.
  - **Focus ring:** every in-page focusable on all 5 pages has a visible outline (no `outline: none` without a replacement).
- **Screen-reader spot check:** not run (Regulated-only per the QA conventions). The AT-facing ACs (H1 name, counters, `lang`, new-tab text) are asserted through the accessibility tree.
